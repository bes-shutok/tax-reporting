## Filing Guidelines: Futures and Derivatives Losses

When reporting futures/derivatives losses from platforms like ByBit, Binance, or OKX:

1. **Report the loss in Anexo J Quadro 9.4** for foreign exchanges: list the platform name, total disposals, and capital gains/losses
2. **Verify holding period**: if the derivative position was held ≥365 days, both gains AND losses are excluded per CIRS art. 10(19)
3. **Document short-term losses for carry-forward**: losses from positions held <365 days can be carried forward for 5 years (PT-C-016) to offset future crypto gains
4. **Track offsetting gains**: record which future gains are offset by carried-forward losses for audit trail
5. **Distinguish liquidation from withdrawal**: a liquidation is a taxable disposal (alienação onerosa) under CIRS art. 10(1)(e); a withdrawal to your own wallet is not a disposal event

**Example:** ByBit SOL/USDT position liquidated on 19 Jan 2025. Report as disposal on ByBit line in Anexo J Quadro 9.4 with negative capital gain amount. If held <365 days, document the loss for 5-year carry-forward; if held ≥365 days, both gain and loss are excluded.
