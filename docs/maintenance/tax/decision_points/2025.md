## Decision Points

### TOML schema (maintainer note)

When copying `2025.toml` for a new fiscal year:

1. A `[countries.XX]` decision-point value may be `bool` OR `dict[str, Decimal]`
   (DP-015 is the first non-boolean field type). The loader type-dispatches over
   `get_type_hints(TaxJurisdictionConfig)`: `hint is bool` -> boolean validation;
   `get_args(hint) == (str, Decimal)` -> dict validation + Decimal conversion.
2. For a `dict[str, Decimal]` field (e.g. `exclude_transaction_fee_max_eur_per_asset`),
   the KEYS are the eligibility set and the VALUES are per-asset ceilings, loaded via
   `Decimal(str(value))` (NOT `Decimal(value)` - the latter ingests binary-float noise).
   Declare it as a TOML subtable `[countries.XX.<field_name>]`.
3. The bool-default loop in the loader touches ONLY bool-typed fields; dict-typed
   fields default to `{}` via the dataclass `default_factory` when absent from the TOML,
   so a country that omits the subtable gets an empty dict with no error.

## Filing Guidelines: Futures and Derivatives Losses

When reporting futures/derivatives losses from platforms like ByBit, Binance, or OKX:

1. **Route by counterparty residency** (per AT binding ruling Processo 28298/2025, despacho 2025-10-31,
   paras 22-23; CIRS art. 10(1)(e)):
   - **Resident counterparty** -> report in **Anexo G, Quadro 13**, operation code **G51**; signal the
     englobamento choice in **Quadro 15**.
   - **Non-resident counterparty** (the usual case for foreign exchanges such as ByBit/Binance/OKX) ->
     report in **Anexo J, Quadro 9.2.B**, operation code **G30**; signal the englobamento choice in
     **Quadro 9.2.C**.
   - (NOT Quadro 9.4, which is "Alienação onerosa de criptoativos" - spot crypto, art. 10(1)(k).)
2. **No 365-day exemption for derivatives.** CIRS art. 10(19) excludes gains/losses only for "operações
   previstas na alínea k)" (spot criptoativos); derivatives fall under alínea e), so **both gains and
   losses are taxable/deductible regardless of holding period** (DP-012, PT-C-034).
3. **Document losses for carry-forward**: derivatives losses can be carried forward for 5 years (PT-C-016,
   CIRS art. 55(1)(d)) to offset future Category G gains, with no holding-period condition.
4. **Track offsetting gains**: record which future gains are offset by carried-forward losses for audit trail.
5. **Distinguish liquidation from withdrawal**: a liquidation is a taxable disposal (alienação onerosa)
   under CIRS art. 10(1)(e); a withdrawal to your own wallet is not a disposal event.

**Example:** ByBit SOL/USDT position liquidated on 19 Jan 2025 (ByBit is non-resident). Report as a
disposal on the ByBit line in **Anexo J Quadro 9.2.B, code G30**, with the negative gain/loss amount, and
carry the loss forward 5 years (PT-C-016). No 365-day exclusion applies to derivatives.
